# UEGT

UEGT is an original, non-profit global strategy and tactical defense game for Unreal Engine 5.8.2 or newer. Its target remains a complete game—not a prototype or vertical slice—with campaign strategy, base management, research, manufacturing, interception, turn-based tactical combat, progression, modding, saving, localization, and release-quality presentation.

OpenXcom is retained in this worktree as a GPL reference catalog for system breadth. UEGT is intentionally not an exact reproduction: production code, data formats, rules, names, narrative, interface, art, audio, and audiovisual content are original. The Unreal targets do not compile, include, link, or load the OpenXcom source or bundled data.

The v0.59 Enduring Beacon pass extends the shared personnel career projection to five exact bands: `First Watch` (0–4), `Field Proven` (5–9), `Long Watch` (10–19), `Legacy Anchor` (20–39), and maximum `Enduring Beacon` (40+). Enduring mentors provide +15 starting morale to lower-band teammates, while the existing Long Watch and Legacy Anchor values remain +5 and +10; Legacy Relay now accepts a maxed-doctrine specialist from Legacy Anchor or higher. Strategic roster, memorial, debrief, and tactical projections share the same derived result, with no persisted field, save migration, random draw, or authored-rule change. One five-culture band label brings the strict catalog to 1,441 entries with 374 exact diagnostics, and all 158 `UEGT.Core` automation tests pass.

## Requirements

- Unreal Engine 5.8.2 or newer
- A Visual Studio toolchain supported by that Unreal installation

The original Unreal code under `Source/` is MIT licensed. OpenXcom reference files retain GPL-3.0-or-later terms, and original X-COM intellectual property is not part of UEGT production content. Non-profit status does not waive third-party license or intellectual-property obligations.

The v0.29 regional-mandate tranche turns regional pressure into an authored diplomatic economy. Three original partner regions begin with distinct support, funding weights, and pressure tolerances; support maps to suspended, strained, committed, and allied contribution tiers. Mission escapes reduce both the targeted partner’s support and recurring contribution, thwarted missions restore support, and month boundaries recover low-pressure partners or penalize excess pressure before applying that month’s funding. Once per partner per month, the player may fund civic relief for stronger support recovery or negotiate a security accord for deeper pressure relief. Both choices are transactional, deterministic, consume no random draw, and expose exact projected funding and rejection reasons through the localized dashboard. State, last-action guards, canonical replay, and v1-v22 migration persist through save format v23.

The interception-withdrawal tranche makes breaking contact a separate, formation-atomic player decision.
